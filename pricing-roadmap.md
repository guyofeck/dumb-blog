# Pricing Roadmap

## Yearly Subscriptions
- Offer an annual billing option alongside monthly plans, priced at a discount versus paying monthly (e.g. equivalent to 2 months free).
- Annual subscribers are billed once per year and renew automatically unless cancelled.
- Plan tiers remain the same between monthly and yearly billing — only the billing cadence and price change.

## Volume-Based Discounts
- Discounts scale with usage or seat count, with tiered thresholds (e.g. more seats/usage unlocks a lower per-unit price).
- Volume discounts can stack with annual billing for further savings.
- Custom/enterprise pricing is available above the highest published tier.

## 14-Day Trial
- New accounts get a 14-day free trial with full access to paid features, no credit card required to start.
- A few days before the trial ends, the user is notified so they can choose a plan.
- If no plan is selected by day 14, the account reverts to a limited/free tier rather than being locked out.
